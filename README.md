# AI Film School

An unofficial, plain-language study guide to making realistic AI films with Claude and Higgsfield, in 10 short lessons.

**Open the guides** (GitHub Pages):

- Home page with both courses: `index.html`
- Part 1, the paid studio workflow: `part-1.html`
- Part 2, Zero-Budget AI Film (Claude alone or with free tools): `zero-budget.html`

## What's inside

- The core idea: AI filmmaking is a loop of prompt → batch → review → fix
- What each tool is for (Claude, Higgsfield Cinema Studio, Soul Cinema, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image, Seedream, Seedance, Voice Change)
- 10 lessons: project setup, style prefix, shot list, characters, locations, props, video generation, acting, editing, teamwork
- A checklist to run before you generate, and a short glossary

## Part 2 at a glance

- Three paths: a film made entirely in code by Claude, a film from free daily AI video credits, or a mix
- A free toolbox: fonts, stock footage, Blender, free voices, music and sound libraries, free editors
- 10 lessons from idea to licences, with two copy-ready prompts
